Privacy & opt-out

Who we contact

Response London sends outreach to UK business contacts — people in their professional capacity at UK limited companies, LLPs and other registered businesses. We do not contact private individuals or sole traders at personal email addresses.

Where the data comes from

Contact details are drawn from publicly available business sources, including company websites, Companies House filings, and professional directories. We verify email addresses with a third-party verification service before sending.

Legal basis

We rely on legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) to send business-to-business commercial communications to corporate subscribers under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) Regulation 22(2), which permits unsolicited email to corporate subscribers without prior consent.

We only process the minimum business contact data required to send the message and manage replies. We do not sell or share this data.

How to opt out

You can opt out at any time. The easiest way is to reply to any email from us with the word no, or unsubscribe, or stop. We'll remove you from our list the same day.

You can also email ahtesham.hassan@bdresponseldn.co.uk and we will remove you.

Data retention

Contact data is retained only while the outbound campaign is active. If you opt out, your email address is added to a suppression list so we don't contact you again. We delete other identifying data within 90 days of the campaign ending.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, email ahtesham.hassan@bdresponseldn.co.uk.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
